What 64ARCS is

A small studio.
Three things built so far.

We build the system a business runs on — orders, stock, dues and collections across its branches, typed once — and we build products of our own.

The first system was built for the founder’s own family business, where it could be tried on a real operation with real money before being offered to anyone else. It is in daily use.

We also build two products of our own. Both are early pilots you can open in a browser now, and each says exactly where it stops.

How we work

Four rules, and what each one means in practice.

The business is the specification.

We sit in your branches and build to how the work actually happens, because the processes and relationships that are yours are usually the reason the business won.

Plain language is part of the work.

If we cannot explain a decision in words you would repeat to your own team, we have not finished thinking about it.

Keep what already works.

Where the standard way of working fits you, it stays — if a spreadsheet is already doing the job, we say so early, not late.

Nothing changes all at once.

Your business keeps trading while we build, and a piece is only replaced after you have watched the new one running beside the original.

The name

64ARCS

The 64 is a nod to computing architecture and 64-bit systems, with personal significance to the founder; the arc is a structure that carries weight while leaving an opening.

Where

India.
Working with businesses elsewhere.

We are based in India and build for businesses wherever they operate.

The ones we work with are mid-sized, have outgrown disconnected software and manual coordination, and do not want to build a large technology department — the kind where a generic product almost fits, and the gap between “almost” and “actually” is where the cost lives.
