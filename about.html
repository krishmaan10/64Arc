What we believe

Six positions we work from.

Architecture before software

What gets built matters less than how the parts relate. A business with a clear architecture can add anything. A business without one accumulates.

The business is the specification

Established companies have processes, relationships and knowledge that are genuinely theirs. Those are not inefficiencies to be standardised away — they are usually the reason the business won.

Coherence beats quantity

The goal is not maximum software. Most businesses already have more systems than they need and less connection than they require.

Intelligence needs context

AI is only as useful as what it can reach. Connected businesses get useful AI; fragmented ones get a chatbot.

Build for the next decade

An ecosystem should make the next capability easier to add, not harder. If each new thing is more painful than the last, the architecture was wrong.

Plain language is part of the work

If we cannot explain a decision in words you would repeat to your own team, we have not finished thinking about it. Complexity belongs in the system, not in the conversation.
