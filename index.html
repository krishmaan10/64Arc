AI + Intelligence

AI becomes useful when it understands the business.

Adding an AI chatbot to disconnected systems does not make a business intelligent. 64ARC connects AI to the actual architecture of the company — its customers, inventory, products, transactions, workflows and knowledge.

User asks Which customers are likely to need replenishment next week?

Result 17 customers identified.
User asks Why did margin decline in the West region last month?

Method

We don't start with software.
We start with how your business actually works.

01 — Understand

Map the business as it is.

Map people, processes, customers, systems, information and bottlenecks — including the work that currently happens in conversations, spreadsheets and habit.

02 — Architect

Decide the structure.

Decide what stays, what changes, what connects, what gets built and where AI belongs. The architecture is agreed before a line of production code is written.

03 — Build

Create the platforms the architecture calls for.

Create the required platforms based on the architecture — not a predetermined product catalogue.

04 — Connect

Make it one ecosystem.

Bring platforms, data and workflows together so information moves without a person carrying it.

05 — Evolve

Keep extending the architecture.

Add new interfaces, capabilities and intelligence as the business grows — attaching to the structure instead of starting another silo.

What it asks of you

A family of technology, at home in the way you already work.
It learns the business before it changes it.

The heaviest part of most technology projects is what they ask of you — meetings, decisions in a language you never chose, a year of your attention. You get the family. Not the wiring behind it.

Words you already use

No jargon, no acronyms, no diagrams to decode. You should be able to repeat our explanation to your staff, word for word. If a word needs explaining before you can decide, it is the wrong word.

Nothing stops

Your business keeps trading while we build. One room at a time — orders, then stock, then the rest — so there is no weekend where everything changes at once.

Sometimes you don't need us

Some problems are a process fix, not a platform. If a spreadsheet is already doing the job well, it stays. Honest advice sometimes costs us the work, and you will hear it early, not late.

You stay the expert

You will never be asked to learn architecture, choose a database or sit through a technical review. And never to make a technical decision dressed up as a business one.
