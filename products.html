Our own pilots

Two things we built
for ourselves.

64ARCS builds business systems for other people, and these two products of its own. Both are early pilots you can open in a browser right now — nothing to install, no sign-in. Neither is finished, and each one below says exactly where it stops.

64Griddle

Pilot

It reads your prompt before you send it and tells you what is missing.

You write a request to an AI, and it answers the question you actually asked — which is rarely the one you meant. 64Griddle reads the draft in the box, marks the places where an assistant would have to guess, and offers wording for each one. You accept or reject every change. Nothing is rewritten behind your back, and the words that end up in the box are yours.

Where this pilot stops

  • It reads only the current textbox. It cannot see earlier messages or read attachments.
  • It uses local rules, not a model. It cannot guarantee a better answer.
  • It never invents names, deadlines, technologies or requirements — it only points at gaps.
  • These checks have not been tested for whether they actually improve a model’s output.
  • Token counts are approximate.
  • Editors for live AI providers are not certified yet.

Your prompt stays on your device — the pilot makes no network requests. What the extension stores, and what host sites still see.

PathWay AI

Pilot

A place for a student to work, inside the boundary their teacher set.

A teacher says what this piece of work is for and what help is allowed. The student writes inside that boundary, with tools that work on their own draft — plan it, test their own reasoning, find a starting reference. Everything they do is kept as a record of the session, shown to the student and the teacher alike. It is a record of how the work happened, not a judgement about who wrote it.

Where this pilot stops

  • Replies are prepared examples. There is no connected generative model in this preview.
  • It is not a personalised tutor and cannot answer personal follow-up questions.
  • It is not an authorship detector. The activity record is not a verdict.
  • Teacher controls are a demonstration. School accounts are not connected.
  • It does not search the web or verify a student’s claims.
  • It cannot produce a finished piece of work.

Work stays in your browser by default. Sharing practice requests is optional and off unless you turn it on — what that sends, and what it does not.
