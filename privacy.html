Privacy note

Short, because we collect very little.

Last updated 7 August 2026

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your name, company, email, and optionally phone, role, sector and a description of what you are trying to solve.

Why we collect it

To reply to your enquiry and, if it goes further, to have an informed conversation about your business. Nothing else.

Who sees it

The people at 64ARC involved in responding to you. We do not sell, rent or share enquiry details with third parties, and we do not add you to a marketing list without your asking.

How long we keep it

For as long as the conversation is active, and a reasonable period afterwards for our own records. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when the page loads. If you would rather that did not happen, the fonts can be self-hosted — tell us and we will.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to hello@64arc.com and we will act on it.

Changes

If this note changes materially, the updated version appears here.
